## Major changes since v1.1.0

### Prove & Show

- frozen Trail Cards v1, Trail Comparison v1, Proof Sessions v1, and Trail Topology v2 surfaces
- exact-byte SHA-256 verification with categorical `VERIFIED`, `REJECTED`, and `UNVERIFIED` states
- deterministic portable proof bundles with fresh local reinspection
- concealed-route protection preserved across cards, comparisons, sessions, topology, and replay
- direct-only lineage with no inferred transitive ancestry

### Replay / Inspection

- local deterministic replay of explicitly supplied trail artifacts
- multi-source import with exact-byte deduplication and stable source ordering
- delegated Proof Session and Trail Comparison inspection without a second proof algorithm
- portable session `MATCH`, `MISMATCH`, and `UNREADABLE` presentation
- portable comparison inspection with fresh derived-output verification
- ephemeral operation with no automatic persistence, accounts, telemetry, or sampler influence

### Mobile hardening

- explicit Branch/Directions empty states with recovery actions
- equal visual weight for Blind Descent and Reveal Route, keeping the instrument non-recommending
- branded accessible local-file selectors with explicit selection status
- readable Trail Ledger controls with unavailable actions disabled and explained
- copy controls for long trail identifiers
